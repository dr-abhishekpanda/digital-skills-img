Why This Session

What NMC Expects From the Foundation Course

NMC's CBME Curriculum 2024 says the Foundation Course should give you the "requisite knowledge, communication (including electronic), technical and language skills" for the MBBS programme.

  • It lists "use of information technology, and artificial intelligence" among the skills to build, with an emphasis on clinico-laboratory communication.
  • It wants computer-skills sessions to be "as interactive as possible". That is why your phones are out.
  • Later, electives such as "Use of Artificial intelligence and computers in Health and Medical Education", clinical photography and the ethical use of technology can take you further.

Source: NMC, Competency Based Medical Education Curriculum 2024 [1].

    Chapter 03

    Build Your Own Digital Library

    Five and a half years of lecture notes, PDFs, slides, papers and certificates.
    Give them one home this week, and you'll find any of them in ten seconds in your final year.

    Set-Up Time: One Evening
    Why Bother

    Where Are Your Files Right Now?

    For most first-years: scattered across WhatsApp chats, the Downloads folder, a pen drive and an old phone.

    • "Which group was that Anatomy PDF in?"
    • "physiology notes final (2) NEW.pdf"
    • A certificate you need today, on a phone you lost last year.

    A digital library fixes this with a few habits: trusted sources, one folder tree, names that sort themselves, a reference manager and backups.

    The Workflow

    From "Found It" to "Can Find It Again"

    Start With Trusted Shelves (next card).
    Your textbooks and the college library, NMC, PubMed, government and WHO guidelines come first. Search engines and social media come later, and only to find a source you can then check.

    Save the File and Where It Came From.
    A PDF without its source is hard to cite and impossible to trust later. Zotero's browser button saves both in one click.

    Rename the Moment You Save.
    Date first (YYYY-MM-DD), then subject, topic, type and version, so files sort themselves in date order. Try the generator below.

    One Folder Tree for All 5½ Years.
    Keep it in one cloud drive (Google Drive or OneDrive) that syncs to your phone and tablet. Build yours with the folder-tree builder below.

    Read With a Pen in Hand.
    Highlight sparingly, write questions in the margin, and keep your notes with the PDF. Zotero's built-in reader stores your highlights and comments with the paper.

    Let Software Format References.
    Zotero's plugin for Word, Docs and LibreOffice inserts citations and builds the reference list. Most medical journals use the Vancouver style.

    Follow the 3-2-1 Rule.
    3 copies of everything, on 2 kinds of storage, with 1 kept away from your room (the cloud counts). See "Back Up or Lose It" below.

      Typing or Handwriting?
      
    What the Evidence Says
    
      
    
        
    
          2014 · Mueller & Oppenheimer [10]
    
          In three studies, students who typed notes on laptops copied the lecture more word for word, and did worse on conceptual questions than students who wrote by hand.
        
    
        
    
          2021 · Urry and Colleagues [11]
    
          Repeating the first study, they again found longer, more word-for-word typed notes, but no quiz advantage for handwriting. A pooled look at eight similar studies showed the same.
        
    
      
    
      
    The Lesson Your device matters less than what your brain does with the lecture. Phone, tablet, laptop or paper: write in your own words, and turn on Do Not Disturb before class.
    
    
    

    
    
      A Method for Paper or Tablet
      
    The Cornell Method
    
      
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    Physiology · Blood Groups (L03)2026-10-22Subject and topic: ________________________Date: ______________
    
        
    
          Cues (Write After Class)
          
    
            
    What decides your ABO group?
    
            
    Which antibodies does group O plasma carry?
    
            
    Why is group O negative blood given in emergencies?
    
            
    Why cross-match before every transfusion?
    
          
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          Notes (In Class, in Your Own Words)
          
    
            
    ABO group = which antigens (A, B, both, none) sit on the red cell surface.
    
            
    Plasma carries antibodies against the antigens you lack: group A has anti-B; group B has anti-A; group AB has neither; group O has both.
    
            
    O negative red cells carry no A, B or RhD antigen, so they are used when there is no time to group the patient.
    
            
    A mismatched transfusion can cause an acute haemolytic reaction.
    
          
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          Summary (Two Lines, Same Evening)
          
    Red-cell antigens decide the ABO group, and plasma carries antibodies against the missing ones. That is why we check compatibility before every transfusion.
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
      
      
        
    1. In Class: notes on the right, in short lines and your own words.
      2. 
        
    2. Same Evening: questions in the cue column, and a two-line summary.
      3. 
        
    3. Revision: cover the notes and answer the cues aloud.
      4. 
      
    
      
    On a Tablet
    Draw two lines on a blank page, or save a Cornell page as a template in your notes app.
    
      
    Devised by Walter Pauk at Cornell University.

      Study the Way Memory Works
      
    Which Study Techniques Actually Work?
    
      
    Dunlosky and colleagues reviewed the evidence on ten common study techniques and rated how useful each one is [13].
    
      
    
        
    High Utility
    Practice testing (quizzing yourself) · Distributed practice (spacing out your study)
    
        
    Moderate Utility
    Elaborative interrogation (asking "why?") · Self-explanation · Interleaved practice (mixing topics)
    
        
    Low Utility
    Rereading · Highlighting · Summarisation · Keyword mnemonics · Imagery for text
    
      
    
      
    Technology's Best Study Job Flashcard and spaced-repetition apps that make you recall. AI can draft the cards; you check every one.

    Delegation Deciding whether, when and how to use AI at all."Should AI do this part, or should I?"1

      Delegation
      
    Green, Amber, Red
    
      
    
        
    Green: Go Ahead
          
    • Explain a concept another way
    • Quiz me on my own notes
    • Plan my revision
    • Outline my seminar (I write the content)
    • Write and explain a spreadsheet formula
    • Check my grammar and show the changes
    
        
    Amber: Verify First
          
    • Chapter summaries
    • Drug doses or guideline facts
    • References
    • Translations meant for patients
    
        
    Red: Don't
          
    • Anything with patient identifiers: name, UHID, photo, report
    • AETCOM reflections, logbook entries, attendance
    • Exam answers
    • Anything you will sign as your own without disclosure

      Save These
      
    Six Prompts Worth Saving
    
      
    
        
    1. Socratic Tutor
    Don't give me the answer. Ask me questions, one at a time, until I can explain why the left recurrent laryngeal nerve is longer than the right.
    
        
    2. Practice Test
    From my notes below, write 10 single-best-answer MCQs with explanations, and tell me which line of my notes each one tests.
    
        
    3. Revision Plan
    Plan 14 days of upper-limb revision, 2 hours a day, with spaced reviews on days 1, 3, 7 and 14.
    
        
    4. Seminar Skeleton
    Suggest a 6-slide outline for a 10-minute seminar on blood groups. Headlines only; I'll write the content.
    
        
    5. Sheet Helper
    Write a Google Sheets formula to count families in column C with more than 5 members, and explain each part.
    Expect: =COUNTIF(C:C,">5")
    
        
    6. Language Coach
    Correct the grammar of my paragraph without changing its meaning, and list every change with a reason.
    
      
    
      
    Each of these keeps you doing the thinking. That is the difference between a tutor and a crutch.
    
    
    

    
    
      Discernment
      
    Check Before You Trust
    
      
    
        
    
          
    47%of 115 references that ChatGPT-3.5 produced for medical articles were made up. Another 46% were real but inaccurate; only 7% were real and correct [17].

      • 
            
    • Check every reference on PubMed: title, authors, journal, year, volume and pages.
      • 
            
    • Fluent writing is not the same as correct writing. Watch for outdated or non-Indian guidance.

      Diligence
      
    Use It Responsibly
    
      
      
        
    • Privacy Never put patient-identifiable data into a public AI tool. India's DPDP Rules, 2025 bring in duties for organisations that handle personal data, most of them from May 2027 [18].
      • 
        
    • Accountability ICMR's 2023 guidelines on AI in health care set out ten principles, including accountability and liability, autonomy, data privacy and validity [19]. NMC's Chairperson has said that professional accountability stays with the doctor [20].
      • 
        
    • Fairness AI can carry bias against groups that are under-represented in its training data. ICMR lists non-discrimination and fairness among its principles.
      • 
        
    • Learning In a field experiment with nearly 1,000 high-school students, those who practised maths with standard GPT-4 scored 17% lower than peers without access once the tool was taken away. A tutor version with safeguards largely prevented this [21].
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      Academic Integrity, AI Edition
      
    Honesty Is the Only Strategy That Works
    
      
      
        
    • Presenting someone else's words or ideas as your own is plagiarism, and an AI counts as someone else.
      • 
        
    • Paraphrasing tools don't turn copied work into your own.
      • 
        
    • Follow each teacher's instructions on AI use. When you're unsure, declare any AI help.
      • 
        
    • The same honesty will one day sign case records, certificates and prescriptions.
      • 
        
    • AI-text detectors often wrongly flag non-native English writing as AI-generated [22]. Being open about how you worked protects you better than any trick.
      •

    AI use: I used ChatGPT to quiz me on my notes and to check the grammar of my final draft. The content, analysis and references are my own, and I checked every fact against my Physiology textbook.
    
      
    Change the tool, the tasks and the source to match what you actually did.

      Medical English · Try It
      
    Build a Medical Word
    
      
    Most medical terms join a root (the body part) to a suffix (what is happening to it). Pick one of each.
    
      
    
        
        
        
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      Medical English Toolkit
      
    Hear It, Say It, Fix It
    
      
      
        
    • Hear It Read Aloud in Word or Edge, or Select to Speak on Android, helps with pronunciation and proofreading.
      • 
        
    • Say It Voice typing and Dictate only work when you speak clearly, which is good practice for the ward.
      • 
        
    • Fix It The Language Coach prompt in Chapter 5 shows every grammar change with a reason.
      • 
        
    • Keep a Glossary In your notes app: the term, how to say it, what it means, and one sentence that uses it.
      • 
        
    • One Spelling System per Document Many Indian textbooks use British forms (haemoglobin, oedema, anaemia, paediatrics); American sources drop the extra letters. Set your spell-checker to English (India) or English (UK).
      • 
      
    
      
    Abbreviations That Hurt Patients Write "units", never "U"; "daily", never "QD". No trailing zero (5 mg, not 5.0 mg), and always a leading zero (0.5 mg, not .5 mg) [23].

      Chapter 08
      
    Stay Safe
    
      
    Almost every scam runs on urgency and fear.
    Learn the pattern once and you'll spot it in any language.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      Scam or Real? · All Messages Are Training Mock-Ups
      
    Five Messages: Decide Before You Tap
    
      
    Mentimeter, Live in Class · Quiz · 5 Questions
    Scam or real? Four messages, then the strongest password.    Voting Closed
    
      
    
        
    
          MOCK-UP
    
          
     WhatsApp · Unknown Number
    
          
    NMC NOTICE: e-KYC for MBBS 2026 admissions closes 6 PM TODAY or your seat is CANCELLED. Verify: nmc-admission-kyc[.]xyz
    
          
    
          
    Urgency, a threat and a strange link. NMC's real website is nmc.org.in, and admission matters come through your college, not a WhatsApp forward.
    
        
    
        
    
          MOCK-UP
    
          
     SMS · VK-SCHLRP
    
          
    Scholarship of ₹25,000 approved. Update bank details in 24 hrs or it lapses: short link
    
          
    
          
    Greed, a deadline and a link that asks for bank details. Check scholarships only on the official portal you applied through, typed in yourself.
    
        
    
        
    
          MOCK-UP
    
          
     Voice Call · +91 Unknown
    
          
    "Department of Telecommunications here. All numbers on your Aadhaar will be disconnected in 2 hours. Press 9."
    
          
    
          
    DoT lists fake KYC updates, impersonated officials and threats to disconnect your numbers among common lures [29]. Hang up, then report it on Sanchar Saathi's Chakshu.
    
        
    
        
    
          MOCK-UP
    
          
     SMS · Your Bank's Usual Sender ID
    
          
    ₹500 debited from A/c XX1234. Not you? Report via your bank app or the number on your card.
    
          
    
          
    No link, and no request for your OTP or PIN. It points you to channels you already trust. Still, check through your bank app, never by replying.
    
        
    
        
    
          MOCK-UP
    
          
     Video Call · "Police Headquarters"
    
          
    A uniformed "officer": "You are under digital arrest. Don't hang up. Transfer ₹50,000 for verification."
    
          
    
          
    "Digital arrest" does not exist in Indian law. No agency questions you on a video call or asks for money. See the next card.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Which password is the strongest?
    
          
    
          
    A four-word passphrase, as long as the words are random and unrelated. Names, birth years, "MBBS" and the college's name are among the first guesses attackers try. Never type your real password into any quiz or page, including this one.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
      
    Red Flags in Any Language
    
      
    
         Urgency Fear or Greed Authority You Can't Check A Link or an App to Install Money, OTP or PIN "Tell No One"
      
    
    
    

    
    
      The Scam to Remember
      
    "Digital Arrest" Does Not Exist
    
      
    In Mann Ki Baat on 27 October 2024, the Prime Minister said that no investigating agency questions people over phone or video calls, or demands money that way. Indian law has no provision for "digital arrest" [30].
    
      
    
        
    StopDon't panic.
    Don't pay.
    
        
    ThinkNo real officer works like this.
    
        
    ActHang up and report.
    
      
    
      
      
        
    • Money Already Gone? Call 1930 or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in straight away. By July 2025, this system had helped save more than ₹5,489 crore [31].
      • 
        
    • No Loss Yet? Report the number on Sanchar Saathi, using its Chakshu facility.
      • 
        
    • The Stakes Indians reported losing ₹22,845 crore to cyber fraud in 2024 [31].
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      Lock Your Accounts
      
    Passwords and Two-Step Verification
    
      
      
        
    • Use a Passphrase Four or more random words beat symbol tricks. NIST's 2025 guidance sets a 15-character minimum for a password used on its own, and drops forced complexity and routine changes [32].
      • 
        
    • Guard Your Email Give your email its own unique password, because every other account resets through it. Let a password manager remember the rest.
      • 
        
    • Never Share an OTP or PIN Not with anyone, including someone who says they are from your bank.
      • 
      
    
      
    Do These Two Tonight
