# Digital Skills for an Indian Medical Graduate

A 90-minute Foundation Course session for MBBS Batch 2026 at Government Medical College & Hospital, Sundaragada, built as one interactive web page instead of a slide deck.

**Open it:** https://dr-abhishekpanda.github.io/digital-skills-img/

This is the student copy. It works offline once saved, and it fits phones, tablets and laptops. Mentimeter voting is switched off on this public copy.

Ten chapters: Start Here · Your MBBS Software Kit · Build Your Own Digital Library · Note-Taking That Works · AI Tools, Used Well · Language & Lab Communication · Be Professional Online · Stay Safe · Stay Well · Your Toolkit.

Lipsa, Anshuman, the case sheets, the FAP data and the mock messages are invented for teaching.

Prepared with AI assistance by Dr. Abhishek Panda, Department of Community Medicine, GMCH Sundaragada.
